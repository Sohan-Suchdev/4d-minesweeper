How to play 4D Minesweeper

You're sweeping a four-dimensional minefield. Each cell touches up to 80 neighbours across the W, X, Y, Z axes — rendered as a 2D grid of 2D sub-grids.

Hover the centre of a 3⁴ board and every other cell — all 80 of them — lights up.

The basics

  • Left-click to reveal · right-click cycles flag, question mark, clear.
  • The first click is always safe — mines are placed afterwards.
  • Win by revealing every non-mine cell. Detonate one and it's over.

Wrap mode

Toggling Wrap stitches the edges of the board together — the grid becomes a hyper-torus. Step off the left edge and you reappear on the right; off the top and you return at the bottom. Every cell, even the corners, now has the full 80 neighbours. The topology folds back on itself: there are no walls in hyperspace.

Delta mode

Each revealed number tells you how many of a cell's 80 neighbours hide a mine. Delta mode subtracts the flags you've already placed from that count — so the displayed number becomes "mines I haven't yet found". When it reaches zero, the surrounding cells are safe to sweep; if it goes negative, you've over-flagged.
